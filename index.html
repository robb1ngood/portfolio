2.1 / Squeak or Sneak


Lead Programmer & Technical Designer | Unity (C#) | 1 Week (Utrecht Summer School)

Developed during an intensive 5-day Game Design & Development program in Utrecht, focusing on rapid prototyping and cross-disciplinary collaboration. The goal was to design and prototype a co-op puzzle game from scratch within five days, centered around the prompt: "Cheese."

Working as a small 3-person team against larger groups of 5, we had to rapidly prototype mechanics that felt polished enough for a "Best Game" showcase. As the primary developer, I focused on the "glue" that made the game playable: scripting player systems for co-op movement, building a modular interactable framework for puzzles, acting as the technical anchor for level integration, and collaborating on puzzle design.

Out of 6 competing teams, Sneak and Squeak won the "Best Game" award. Despite being the smallest team, we delivered the most stable and mechanically complete experience, proving our ability to prioritize "fun factor" and technical stability under a tight deadline.

2.2 / Doodle Run


Solo Developer (Design, Art, & Logic) | Construct 3 | 1 Week

Doodle Run is a high-precision 2D platformer focused on "hard parkour" mechanics, challenging the player through timed jumps and rhythmic movement. The primary hurdle was balancing "punishingly difficult" gameplay with "fair" mechanics, ensuring a failure felt like a skill error rather than a glitch.



As a solo creator, I managed every pillar of production:


  • Mechanics: Developed movement and jump using Construct’s event system.
  • Art Direction: Created character sprites and environmental assets inspired by the game BattleBlock Theater, communicating "danger" vs. "safety".
  • Level Design: Iterated on level layouts to introduce mechanics gradually before combining them into high-intensity sequences.
  • Enemy Logic: Scripted patrol behaviours and hazard triggers to add layers of complexity.

The result is a fully playable, end-to-end platforming experience that gave me a deep understanding of how interactions affect player frustration and satisfaction.
