01 / About Me

My name is Márk Mucska, and I am a computer science student in my final year at the University of Pécs. My focus is on creating games that leave an impact on the player.

I have worked with Unity and Godot, and I am currently dedicated to mastering the production pipeline. In my free time, I enjoy video games, origami, and writing Steam reviews.

Sneak and Squeak

Lead Programmer & Technical Designer | Unity | Best Game Award

Developed during an intensive 5-day program in Utrecht, our team designed a co-op puzzle game based on the theme "Cheese". As the Lead Programmer, I acted as the technical anchor for the project.
